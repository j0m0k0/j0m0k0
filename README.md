## About Me
I’m a 4th-year graduate student in the CS department at the Texas State University. My research lies at the intersection of AI security, reinforcement learning and agentic AI. I specialize in analyzing the security of emerging threats in underlying layers of an AI system such as a LLM-based multi-agent system and design efficient defensive models against those threats. My goal is to develop practical, high-performance defenses that secure the systems that rely on artificial intelligence such as critical infrastructures.
